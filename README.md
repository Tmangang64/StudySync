# StudySync

StudySync is a web-based academic accountability app designed to help college students manage study sessions, track progress, build consistent habits, and stay accountable through solo, buddy, and group study workflows.

## Project Overview

This project was created for **CSC 360: Interface Design**. StudySync was designed to solve a common student problem: many students plan to study, but often struggle to follow through because of distractions, weak routines, time conflicts, and lack of accountability.

The app combines planning, check-ins, recovery support, progress tracking, and collaborative study features into one interface so students can stay organized and motivated.

## Problem Statement

College students often intend to study, complete assignments, and stay ahead of deadlines, but many fail to follow through consistently. Existing tools may help with scheduling or communication, but they do not always provide the structure, reminders, accountability, and recovery support students need.

StudySync was designed to address this gap by helping students:
- commit to study sessions
- check in and complete sessions
- recover after missed goals
- monitor streaks and weekly progress
- work with accountability partners or groups

## Core Features

- **Login simulation**
- **Dashboard** with upcoming sessions, recent activity, and accountability information
- **Commit screen** for planning study sessions
- **Check-in workflow** for starting and managing sessions
- **Progress tracking** with weekly goals, streaks, and study history
- **Recovery flow** for missed sessions
- **Buddy and group study support**
- **Reference notes upload**
- **Auto-generate study materials** such as flashcards, quiz questions, study guides, and reflection prompts
- **Saved data persistence** across logins
- **Supportive motivational feedback** to encourage students throughout the study process
